Levain Log

Privacy policy

Effective November 19, 2026

Levain Log is a sourdough starter and bread tracker. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into it.

The short version

Levain Log keeps everything on your phone. It has no accounts, no servers, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer never receives your data. If you turn on backups to Google Drive, they go to your own Google Drive and nowhere else.

What the app stores

Everything you enter stays in the app's private storage on your device:

  • your starters, feedings, rise readings and notes
  • bake plans, bake journal entries and your own recipes
  • photos you take or choose for feedings, bakes and recipes (the app keeps its own copy)
  • your settings, such as units, color scheme and the jar character's outfits

None of this is sent to the developer or to anyone else, except to your own Google Drive if you turn on backups (below).

Permissions

The app only asks for these when you use the feature that needs them:

  • Camera: to take a photo of a bake, feeding or recipe.
  • Photos: to choose an existing picture for the same purpose. Only the pictures you pick are used.
  • Notifications: for feeding reminders and bake-step alerts. These are scheduled on your phone; no notification server is involved.

You can turn any of these off in your phone's settings. The rest of the app keeps working.

Purchases

Levain Log Plus is an optional one-time purchase. Google Play (or the App Store on iPhone) handles the payment, under their own privacy policy. The app only asks the store whether your account owns Plus, and remembers the answer on your phone. Levain Log never sees your payment details.

Sharing

When you share a card or a CSV export of your feedings, the app hands it to your phone's share sheet. It goes only to the app or person you choose, and what happens next is up to that app's own privacy policy.

Backups to your Google Drive

Backups are optional and off until you turn them on in Settings → Backup. When you sign in with Google to turn them on:

  • Levain Log asks only for access to the files it creates in your Google Drive. It can't see, open or change anything else in your Drive.
  • It saves backups of your data and photos in a "Levain Log" folder in your Drive, about once a day when you open the app, and keeps the last few. It reads them back only when you choose to restore one.
  • The backups go straight from your phone to your Google Drive. They are never sent to the developer or anyone else, and they are not used for advertising or anything other than backing up and restoring your data.
  • The app remembers your Google account's email address on your phone, to show which account it backs up to.
  • Stopping backups in Settings → Backup signs out and removes the app's access. Backups already in your Drive stay there until you delete them; you can delete the "Levain Log" folder in Google Drive at any time.

Levain Log's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can also save a copy of your data as a file yourself. It goes only where you choose to save or send it.

Your phone's own backup

If you have turned on your phone's own backup (for example Google's device backup on Android or iCloud on iPhone), your phone may include Levain Log's data in it. That backup is handled by your phone's maker under their privacy policy, not by Levain Log.

Deleting your data

  • Settings → Your data → Erase all data deletes everything the app stores on your phone.
  • Uninstalling the app also deletes it.
  • Backups in Google Drive are deleted by deleting the "Levain Log" folder in your Google Drive.

Because the developer never has a copy, there is nothing else to delete.

Children

Levain Log is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
